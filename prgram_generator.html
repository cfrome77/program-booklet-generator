Lodge Chief's Welcome

Lodge Chief

Welcome, Arrowmen, families, and honored guests! Tonight marks an extraordinary milestone as we celebrate 75 years of fellowship and leadership. Thank you for your relentless dedication to our camps and chapters.

[ Adviser Photo ]

Lodge Adviser's Message

Lodge Adviser

For three-quarters of a century, the strength of Amangamek-Wipit Lodge has lived in the character of its youth and steady guidance of its scouters. Let us celebrate our past while building our next chapter together.

Page 3
Keynote Speaker
[ Speaker Photo ]

Max Mihalov

Eastern Region Chief

Eagle Scout and Vigil Honor member Max Mihalov (Paugassett Lodge, Section E20) studies finance at Fordham University. He previously served as Lodge Chief, Conclave Vice Chief, and two terms as Section Chief.

As Eastern Region Chief, Max supports regional lodges and facilitates National Leadership Seminars and Developing Youth Leadership Conferences. He is especially excited for Operation Arrow at the National Jamboree, eager to meet thousands of Scouts from across the country.
Page 4
Year in Review & Outlook

Journey to Excellence Gold

Lodge 470 achieved Gold Standard recognition through high-impact camping promotion, service hours, and quality brotherhood events across council properties.

Looking Ahead to 2027

Join us May 14–16, 2027 at Goshen Scout Reservation for Spring Fellowship, and look for upcoming OA Corps summer camp service deployments across local camps.

Lodge & Special Honors

New Arrowman Award

Recognizes newly inducted Ordeal members who rapidly engage in lodge activities within their first year.

Key Three Chapters

Recognizes outstanding chapter leadership, unit support, and collaborative program execution.

PMP Award

Performance Measurement Program standard achieved through structured lodge operations.

Unit of Excellence

Honors units maintaining high standards of camping, service, and youth leadership.

Young Adviser's Award

Ian McCuistion Award honoring adult advisers (ages 21–25) for energetic peer mentorship.

[Medallion]

Servant Leadership Award

Honors Arrowmen who consistently prioritize the needs of others, laboring behind the scenes without expectation of applause.

[Medallion]

Founder’s Award & History

Honors members patterning daily service after E. Urner Goodman and Carroll A. Edson.
Recent: C. Frome, D. Smith, A. Clark, L. Martinez, S. Harris

Vigil Honor

The Highest Brotherhood

The Vigil Honor is the highest honorary membership in the Order of the Arrow, recognizing unselfish service well beyond normal expectations. Members are recognized by their unique totem and lifetime dedication.

“Recognizing those inducted into the highest brotherhood of unselfish service across our lodge's history.”
2027 Vigil Class Honoree
